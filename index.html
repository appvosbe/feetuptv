FeetUpTV Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

FeetUpTV is a media player for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What we collect

Nothing. FeetUpTV has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer doesn't receive any information about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Provider logins (playlist links, server addresses, usernames and passwords) are stored only on your iPhone, in the iOS Keychain.
  • Favorites, recently watched items, settings and downloaded channel lists are stored only on your iPhone.

Removing a provider in Settings, or deleting the app, removes this information. The same policy is in the app under Settings → Privacy Policy.

Who the app talks to

  • Your IPTV provider. To show and play your channels, FeetUpTV connects directly to the servers you entered, using the login you gave it. Your provider's own privacy policy applies to those connections.
  • Your TV. When you use AirPlay or Chromecast, the app sends the stream link to the TV you choose on your local network. To find Chromecasts, iOS asks for permission to look for devices on your local network.
  • Apps you choose. "Open in VLC / Infuse" hands the stream link to that app.
  • Sample videos. If you use them, they stream from Apple's and the Blender Foundation's public servers.

FeetUpTV doesn't provide any channels or content, and doesn't send your information anywhere else.

Children

FeetUpTV isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
