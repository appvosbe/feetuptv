FeetUpTV Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email app@vosbe.com.

Getting started

FeetUpTV is a player: it doesn't come with channels. Add your own provider on the first screen, either with an M3U link or an Xtream login (server, username and password). To look around first, tap Try with sample videos.

A channel won't play

iPhone plays HLS and MP4 streams. For other formats, such as MKV movies, use Open in VLC or Infuse. If the error screen shows a "Details" line, include it in your email; it says what went wrong.

Watching on a TV

Use the AirPlay button for Apple TV and AirPlay TVs, or the Chromecast button for Chromecast and Google TV. Most providers allow one stream at a time, so the app may need a few seconds to hand the stream over to the TV.
